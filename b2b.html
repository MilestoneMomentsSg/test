FAQ

Common questions
from coordinators

What is the minimum order size for institutional pricing?

Institutional pricing starts from 10 sets. Orders below this threshold can still be placed through our standard student booking flow, though at individual rental rates.

How far in advance should we place our order?

We recommend at least 4 weeks before the ceremony date for standard orders. For orders of 200+ sets or multi-date events, 6–8 weeks ensures we can accommodate all requirements. We do accept late requests where stock allows.

Do you support faculty-specific hood and gown colours?

Yes — please include your faculty colour requirements in the enquiry form or notes field. We'll confirm availability when we send your quote. Custom or non-standard colours may require additional lead time.

What payment methods are accepted for institutional orders?

We accept bank transfer (FAST/GIRO), cheque, and PayNow for institutional accounts. Purchase orders can be raised in advance; a tax invoice is issued upon delivery. Please mention your preferred payment method in your enquiry.

What happens if gowns are damaged or not returned?

Standard orders include a per-item refundable deposit. For enterprise accounts, we offer a damage waiver option that covers normal wear and minor incidents. Full details are outlined in the institutional rental agreement.
